Customer Tags in Sale Order

Your customer's tags on every quotation and sales order, without opening the contact.

Odoo 17 Community & Enterprise AGPL-3

Highlights

Tags on the order

The quotation and sales order form shows the customer's tags, in their colours, right under the Customer field.

Always current

The tags are read from the customer: change them on the contact and every order of that customer shows the new ones.

Has tags filter

A new filter in the contacts search lists the customers that have at least one tag.

See who you are selling to

Salespeople see at a glance that a customer is a VIP, a reseller or has payment issues, while they prepare the quotation. The field is read-only on the order and hidden when the customer has no tags, so the form stays clean.

How to use

  1. Install Customer Tags in Sale Order from Apps.
  2. Open a customer and fill in its Tags field.
  3. Open or create a quotation for that customer: the tags appear under Customer.
  4. To list the customers that have tags, search the contacts (Contacts, or Sales › Orders › Customers) and pick Filters › Has tags.

Technical details

  • Depends on: Sales (sale).
  • Adds a non-stored related field partner_tags (customer's category_id) to sale.order; no new table or column.
  • Extends the sales order form (sale.view_order_form) and the contacts search view (base.view_res_partner_filter).
  • Creates no data on install and removes none on uninstall.
  • Translations: Ukrainian.
