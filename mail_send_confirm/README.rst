In the chatter, *Send message* emails the message to the followers of the
record, while *Log note* keeps it internal. Odoo sends at once, without asking,
so a note meant for colleagues sometimes reaches the customer.

This module asks for a confirmation before a chatter message is emailed to
someone outside the company, and lists exactly who will receive it.

Features:

* A confirmation dialog with the name and email address of every external
  recipient, with *Send* and *Cancel*
* Covers the *Send* button and Ctrl+Enter in the chatter, and *Send* in the
  full composer opened from the chatter
* Recipients are computed on the server the way Odoo computes them when it
  posts the message: followers subscribed to discussions, added and suggested
  recipients, mentioned contacts, new email addresses; the author and archived
  contacts are left out
* External means a contact without an internal user: customers, vendors,
  portal users
* No dialog when only internal users are notified, and never for log notes
* A company setting: confirm for external recipients (default), for any email
  recipient, or never

Usage
=====

To configure:

1. Go to **Settings > General Settings**, section **Emails**,
   setting **Confirm Emailed Messages**
2. Choose when to ask, for the current company:

   * **External recipients** (default): when at least one contact without an
     internal user will receive the message by email
   * **Any email recipient**: also when only internal users receive it by
     email (users notified in Odoo are not counted)
   * **Never**: send without asking

To use:

1. Open a record with a chatter, for example a contact or a quotation
2. Click **Send message**, write the message and click **Send**
   (or press Ctrl+Enter)
3. If the message will be emailed to external contacts, a dialog lists them.
   Click **Send** to post it, or **Cancel** to go back to the message,
   which is kept as it is

The same check runs when you click **Send** in the full composer (the expand
icon of the chatter composer); **Cancel** keeps the composer open.

Not covered: the *Send by email* buttons of documents (quotations, invoices,
...), which are meant to email the customer, scheduled messages and edited
messages.
